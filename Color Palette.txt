5. Implementation Tips for Codesys
When setting up your Codesys Visualization Manager or Styles, follow these rules to maintain the "Professional Industrial" look:

Fonts: Use Arial, Segoe UI, or Tahoma. Keep them Bold for numeric values. Do not use serif fonts.

Contrast Ratio: Ensure the contrast between text and background is at least 4.5:1. The suggested Dark Concrete (#1E1E1E) with White text (#FFFFFF) has a ratio of ~15:1, which is excellent for factory floor glare.

Touch Targets: If using a touch panel, keep buttons large. Use the Surface (Elevated) color for default buttons and Corporate Blue for primary actions (e.g., "Start Cycle").

Animation: Use Signal Yellow for blinking only for "Unacknowledged Alarms" or "Ready to Start." Avoid blinking for normal operations.
